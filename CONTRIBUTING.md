# Contributing to Hybrid RAG Systems & AI From Scratch

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Hybrid RAG Systems**! This repository is an open-source educational companion to **[AI From Scratch](https://www.aifromscratch.in/)**, a platform dedicated to making cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence practical, intuitive, and accessible to everyone.

---

## 🌟 How You Can Contribute

We welcome contributions of all kinds:
1. **Reporting Bugs & Issues**: Found a bug in a notebook or script? Open a detailed GitHub issue with reproduction steps.
2. **Improving Explanations**: Notice an intuition or derivation that could be clearer? Feel free to propose PRs that improve educational clarity.
3. **Optimizing Algorithms**: Enhance the performance or readability of BM25, Reciprocal Rank Fusion, or embedding routines.
4. **Extending Tutorials**: Propose new benchmarks, document loaders, or evaluations.

---

## 🛠️ Development Guidelines

1. **First-Principles Philosophy**: When submitting code, prioritize pure Python, NumPy, and clean abstractions before introducing external heavy dependencies.
2. **Documentation & Readability**: Keep comments informative. Every function must include clear docstrings and typing.
3. **Local Testing**: Verify that notebooks execute cleanly from start to finish without errors (`python verify_setup.py`).
